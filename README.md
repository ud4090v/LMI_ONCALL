The On Call is a task that everyone currently assigned to our team works on. While quite different than what most of us do in our day to day, it is not a task that Basis does but rather work that we do during specific “shifts” throughout the year. Most of us have 3 to 4 shifts in a year as Primary (and 3 to 4 more as backup) where, during that shift, we can be called at any time to assist/deal with issues related to Control M jobs. Each shift lasts a week and during that week calls can come in at any hour (and quite frankly, most come in the middle of the night when we are sleeping). It is important to pick up those calls as failing to do so escalates the call. Not only that, but the calls are usually due to failures of critical Control-M jobs so getting them addressed is important.  Attached is a document with an overview of the process of what to do when you are called. You should have all required accesses as they should have been requested for you a while back.
